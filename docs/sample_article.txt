Coral Bay Athletic 2-1 Granite Hill FC: Late Melo header settles a tense night at Reef Stadium

Coral Bay Athletic came from behind to beat Granite Hill FC 2-1 in front of 21,400 fans at Reef Stadium on Saturday, with Leandro Melo heading the winner in the 84th minute.

The visitors started brighter and went ahead in the 17th minute when Conor Walsh curled a free kick past Rafael Souza. Granite Hill held the lead until half-time, but Coral Bay dominated after the break, finishing the match with 61% possession and 15 shots to Granite Hill's 6.

The equaliser arrived in the 58th minute. Bruno Lima's low cross was turned in at the near post by Gabriel Nunes for his fifth goal of the season. Granite Hill were reduced to ten men in the 71st minute when Aidan Byrne was sent off for a second yellow card.

With six minutes left, Andre Viana swung in a corner and Leandro Melo rose highest to head home. Souza then made a fine save from Shane Daly in stoppage time to protect the lead.

"We were patient and we believed until the end," said Coral Bay head coach Carlos Mendes. "Leandro has been waiting for a night like this."

The win lifts Coral Bay Athletic to third in the table, four points behind the leaders.
